Read video metadata without ffmpeg.

Drop a file, pick a sample stream, or paste a URL. You get resolution, codec, framerate, HDR, rotation, bit depth and every audio and subtitle track the container declares. It reads about a megabyte of headers and stops, whether the source is a URL or a file you drop here, so a 40 GB video costs roughly what a 4 MB one does.

Worth knowing

    

      

        
Why some URLs fail here

        

          A browser can only fetch a URL whose host sends
          Access-Control-Allow-Origin. Hosts also need to expose
          Content-Range, or an MP4 storing its moov at the end
          cannot be read. Node has neither restriction.
        

      

      

        
Local paths are server-only

        

          getVideoResolution("/path/to/video.mp4") needs a filesystem.
          In the browser you pass a File, Blob or
          ReadableStream instead, which is what the drop zone does.
        

      

      

        
Dimensions are coded, not displayed

        

          A portrait phone clip reports 1920×1080 with
          rotation: 90. Swap the axes yourself when rotation is 90 or 270.
